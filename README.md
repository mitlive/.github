<h3 align="center">A small group of builders and tinkerers</h3>

<p align="center">
  Networking, connected hardware, healthcare access, accessibility,<br>rural connectivity and public safety
</p>

---

Most of what is here started because someone had a problem worth solving. A campus running on ageing infrastructure. A consulate buried in paperwork. A village with no usable bandwidth. Someone who needs to be heard without typing a sentence first.

We pick problems where the constraint is real, ship something that holds up, and keep it running.

## Projects
